Clarity & Compliance

Essential answers before you start.

We provide transparent legal advisory for UK, European, and international entrepreneurs looking to establish a tax-efficient corporate presence in the UAE.

Why is the pricing displayed in British Pounds (£/GBP)?

Many of our executive international and UK-based clients manage their treasury in Sterling. We convert the statutory UAE fees and include our dedicated London/Dubai legal management fee (+£2,500) so you have an exact, all-inclusive number without hidden currency fluctuations.

What is included in the £2,500 Professional Setup & Legal Compliance Fee?

This fee covers complete end-to-end concierge handling: drafting Memorandum of Association (MOA), board resolutions, shareholder verification, power of attorney filings, immigration establishment card coordination, and dedicated case manager oversight until your corporate bank account is open.

Can I obtain 100% foreign ownership?

Yes. All 9 free zones featured in our calculator permit 100% foreign corporate or individual ownership without requiring a local UAE national sponsor or partner.

Do I need to travel to the UAE to register the company?

No. The company licence registration can be conducted entirely remotely. Travel to the UAE is only required for 3–5 working days if you apply for personal UAE Residence Visas (for mandatory medical biometrics and Emirates ID stamping).

How does the LocalStorage feature work in this calculator?

Every step you take, option you toggle, and activity you select is automatically stored securely in your browser's LocalStorage. If you reload or close the tab, your complete setup is instantly restored so you never lose your progress.
